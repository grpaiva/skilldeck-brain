An AI assistant cites a brand for a topic when the brand's own site has a page that answers that topic directly. This page lists every topic we want to be cited for and where its page lives, so the gaps are visible. It follows the GEO and SEO plan of November 2026.

Coverage on 2026-11-16: 1 of 8 categories and 11 of 58 Skills have a post on The Feedback.

That is 7 category posts and 47 Skill posts to write.

### Proposed writing order

1. The 7 category posts first. Each one gives its Skills a parent page and a breadcrumb, and a category with no post leaves its Skill posts without one.
2. Skills shared by two categories next, because one post fills a gap in both: Feedback, Empathy, Stress Management, Emotional Balance, Resilience, Curiosity, Objectivity.
3. Then one category at a time, in the row order above. Communication, Emotional Intelligence and Interpersonal Relations come first because they sit closest to feedback and people development, the topics the homepage leads with.

This order is a judgment, not data. Once Search Console and the monthly AI answer check show which topics people ask about, reorder by that.
