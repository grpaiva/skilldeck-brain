Use questions tied to surfaces you can both see — or that the member can open. A question anchored to something on screen produces a specific answer. A general question produces a general one.

## From Weekly Prep and Kickoff · Journal

- "Which recommended topic will you practice this week — and in which meeting?"
- "What does 'good' look like for this Skill in your next customer or team conversation?"
- "What will you try differently than last week?"

## From Weekly Review and Wrap-Up · Journal

- "What progressed vs Goal, and what didn't show up in evidence?"
- "This Track needs at least two weeks — are we giving it enough reps, or are we restless to switch?"
- "If we wrap this Track, what's the next Skill that matters for the role?"
- "Was the Goal too easy, too hard, or right — and why?"

## From Track analyses · Overview

- "Walk me through that meeting. Where did the Skill show up?"
- "What would you do differently next time in the first five minutes?"
- "Who else in the room affected how you showed up — and how will you handle that next time?"

## From the Deck

- "Where are we guessing vs knowing?" — for low confidence.
- "Which Skills around this Track are dragging confidence down?"
- "If we only improved one related Skill this Cycle, which one moves the role most?"

## From Goal stalls

- "Is the Goal wrong, or is there not enough evidence in real situations?"
- "Are we practicing in the wrong meetings?"
- "Should we keep going on this Track, adjust the Goal, or switch Skill — and what's the reason?"

## Accountability — ask these at every level

<Warning>
  These two close every coaching meeting, regardless of whether you run Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
</Warning>

- "What meetings should Skill Deck see between now and our next conversation?"
- "What will you say no to so this Skill gets reps?"
