These are the guiding questions for the [fifteen-minute conversation](/playbooks/manager-coaching/monthly) in Skill Deck. Pick the ones that fit. Ten minutes won't hold all of them.

## Most Demonstrated Skills

- "The skill that came up most this month was X. How do you see that?"
- "Why do you think it came up so much?"
- "What was going on in those meetings that pulled for it?"

## Growth Opportunities

- "This came up as your clearest growth opportunity this month. Do you agree with that?"
- "After you got this feedback, were you able to try something different in a later meeting?"
- "If yes, how did it go? If no, what got in the way?"

## Top Strengths

- "This showed up consistently and well across the month. Were you aware of it?"
- "Is this something you do deliberately, or does it just happen?"

## Best Moments

- "These were your highest-rated moments this month. What was happening there?"
- "Why do you think it came out right then?"
- "What did you draw on to get there?"

## Advisor Pick

You listened to it in step 3 of [How to prepare](/playbooks/manager-coaching/how-to-prepare), so ask whether it landed.

- "Did you listen to the Advisor Pick this month?"
- "What technique was it teaching?"
- "Did you try it in a real meeting? How did it go?"
- "If you didn't try it, what got in the way?"

## Reflections

- "Did this show up again in the later meetings, or did it disappear?"
- "What changed along the way?"
