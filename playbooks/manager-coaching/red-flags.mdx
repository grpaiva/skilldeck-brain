Every red flag below is visible from the team page or the member page today. None of them requires a report or a request.

| Red flag | What you see | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No open Track** | Empty team member row or timeline | Start a Track. Confirm unlocks exist. Check that Tracks are enabled. |
| **Pending Skill or Goal** | Pending status on the member | Configure the Track this week. Do not leave it pending across a Weekly meeting. |
| **Unlocking for too long** | No unlocks, low confidence | Push calendar connection and meeting coverage. Discuss which situations create evidence. |
| **No recent evaluations** | Flat progress, confidence falling | Fix integrations and bot coverage. Agree on concrete meetings to capture. |
| **Stale or expired Track** | Expired, or no movement | Restart with a clearer Skill or a more realistic Goal. Check evidence volume. |
| **Repeated switches, few completions** | Many switched Tracks, few that lived two weeks or more | Slow down the switches. Protect the minimum duration. Coach practice quality, not novelty. |
| **Long Track with no progress** | Same Track for weeks, flat percentage | Diagnose evidence quality and situation fit *before* switching. |
| **Cycle ending at zero completions** | Weak Cycle stats | Run a Cycle review now. Temporarily move that member to Monthly or Weekly. |
| **Wrong Skill for the role** | Track Skill off-whitelist or off-role | Edit the Track. Tighten the team or member whitelist. |

## The escalation rule

<Warning>
  If a Quarterly or Monthly member hits **two red flags in a row**, move them to the next more frequent level — Quarterly → Monthly → Weekly — for one Cycle, then reassess.
</Warning>

The level is a dial, not an identity. Turning it up for a Cycle is the intended response to a member who is stalling, and turning it back down afterward is expected.

## The pattern underneath

Most of these flags come down to one of two causes, and the fix differs sharply.

**Not enough evidence.** No unlocks, no recent evaluations, falling confidence, a Cycle with nothing to discuss. The fix is always upstream: calendar connection, bot coverage, and which meetings the person actually takes. Changing the Skill will not help.

**Not enough patience.** Repeated switches, few completions, Tracks that never live two weeks. The fix is to stop switching. A long Track with flat progress is a diagnosis problem, not a reason to move on — check evidence quality and situation fit first.
