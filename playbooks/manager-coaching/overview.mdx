Skill Deck assesses soft skills from the meetings your team already has, so every score rests on evidence captured in real work. That gives you a lot of signal on each person. This playbook covers what to do with it: fifteen minutes a month, per employee.

## The short version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Book the time">
    Book 15 minutes a month with each person on your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Focus Skills">
    Set 3 to 5 Focus Skills for each of them, on the team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare">
    Before each conversation, read the person's Deck, skim their Journal and open the Monthly Report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the findings">
    Walk through the month's findings in the Report, then talk about one Advisor Pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close">
    Close by agreeing what mattered this month, and name one behavior to practice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on the Focus Skills">
    Decide what you do with the Focus Skills. They can stay or change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The monthly conversation

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Time** | 15 minutes per employee, once a month |
| **Where it lives** | A 15-minute conversation on its own, or the last 15 minutes of a monthly 1:1 |
| **Why it's good for you** | Visibility on how your team actually works, what they're running into, and which skills to build next. Feedback is back on your agenda. |
| **Why it's good for them** | Feedback on real work, and development that happens on the job. |

It's a fifteen-minute segment with a fixed structure. Your performance review and your 1:1 stay as they are.

Monthly works because a month gives the person enough meetings for a pattern to form, and enough distance for you to check whether last month's feedback changed anything.

## One page to keep

**Monthly, 15 minutes, per employee.**

Before: Deck → Focus Skills → Journal → Monthly Report.

In the room:

1. "What from the last month do you want to talk about?"
2. Most Demonstrated Skills. Why so much of this, this month?
3. Growth Opportunities. Did last month's feedback carry forward?
4. Top Strengths and Best Moments. What did you draw on?
5. Advisor Pick. Did you listen to it, and did you use the technique?

Leave with: what mattered, one behavior and where it gets practiced, and what happens to the Focus Skills.
