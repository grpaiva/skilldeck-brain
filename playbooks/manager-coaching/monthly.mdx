<Info>
  Level 02 of the [Manager Coaching Playbook](/playbooks/manager-coaching/overview).
</Info>

**About 45 to 60 minutes, once a month.** Roughly three meetings per Cycle.

Best when you have a steady team of mixed seniority, or limited manager bandwidth.

## Who owns the loop

The member runs the weekly Track autopilot. The manager spots patterns and redirects.

## Fit with Tracks

Between meetings the member may still be on the same Track, with the Goal not met yet. Or you may see a recent Wrap-Up and a new Kickoff.

<Tip>
  **Mindset.** Do not treat "still on Track A" as failure. A Track that lives across two monthly conversations is often the Track that actually changed behavior.
</Tip>

## Meeting agenda — about 45 to 60 minutes

| Time | Topic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | The month in one sentence | Member |
| 10 min | Tracks since the last meeting — progress, completion, or why still active | Overview / team timeline |
| 15 min | Patterns across Preps and Reviews, plus the Advisor topics used | Journal |
| 10 min | Deck movement and confidence quality | Deck |
| 10 min | Until next month: keep the Goal, switch the Skill (with a reason), or raise the Goal. Plus the evidence plan. | Overview, and Track Settings if needed |
| 5–10 min | Optional: Target and role expectations | Targets, if used |

## Between meetings

- A light mid-month Journal check, only if progress looks flat.
- Intervene early on red flags — no Track, expired or stale Track, zero evaluations.
- Prefer auto-assigning the Skill and Goal, so the member is not blocked waiting on you.

That last point is the one Monthly managers most often get wrong. At this cadence, a member who needs your decision to start a Track loses weeks.

## Success signals

  </Card>
  <Card title="A clear next month" icon="check">

  </Card>

## Red flags

- A month of Journal silence or inactivity.
- An Active Track with no score movement and thin evidence.
- Cycle progress lagging peers on the team timeline.
- The member is unclear what Skill they are practicing.
- Churn: many switches, few Tracks that lived past two weeks.
