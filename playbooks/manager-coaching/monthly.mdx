The monthly conversation in Skill Deck is 15 minutes with one person. You arrive with the Deck read and the Monthly Report open, following [How to prepare](/playbooks/manager-coaching/how-to-prepare).

## Open by letting them choose

> "What from the last month do you want to talk about?"

Let them go first, because what they name is what they've been thinking about. Then bring what you saw in the Report.

## Run it finding by finding

Go through the Report one finding at a time. Each finding adds up the whole month, so stay on the pattern and use a single meeting only as an example.

| Minutes | Focus | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1 | Open: what they want to talk about | Report |
| 1 to 6 | **Most Demonstrated Skills** and **Growth Opportunities** | Report |
| 6 to 11 | **Top Strengths** and **Best Moments** | Report |
| 11 to 13 | **Advisor Pick**: the technique, and whether it got used | Advisor |
| 13 to 15 | Close: skills reflections, and what happens to the Focus Skills | Deck |

Ten minutes covers the findings, as long as you stay on the questions and resist reading the Report back to them.

<Card title="Guiding questions" icon="message-circle-question" href="/playbooks/manager-coaching/coaching-prompts">
  Questions for each finding, the Advisor Pick, and the reflections.
</Card>

## Close

Two things get settled here.

**What mattered.** Agree on what came out of the month, then name one behavior to practice and the meetings where it gets practiced between now and next month.

**What happens to the Focus Skills.** Most months they stay, and you say so out loud. [Keeping or changing Focus Skills](/playbooks/manager-coaching/focus-skills#keeping-or-changing-focus-skills) covers the cases where you change them.
