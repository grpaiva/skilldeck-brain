<Info>
  Level 01 of the [Manager Coaching Playbook](/playbooks/manager-coaching/overview).
</Info>

**About 30 minutes, every week.**

Best when you are working on critical Skills, coaching junior members, onboarding a team that is new to Skill Deck, or running a small team.

## Who owns the loop

The manager steers practice week to week. The member executes in real meetings.

## Fit with Tracks

You will usually hold **several weekly meetings on the same Track**. Treat weeks one and two as build-up. Completion is possible from the second Friday onward, and only if the Goal is met.

## Best day to meet

**Friday.** Hold the coaching meeting after the member receives their Weekly Review — or their Wrap-Up, if the Goal and the minimum duration have both been met.

## Prep checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Member page → Overview">
    Progress vs Goal. Days or weeks on this Track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Journal">
    This week's Prep and Review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track analyses">
    Pick one or two to discuss.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deck">
    Check the Skills with confidence over 30% around the Track Skill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your default bias">
    Keep the Track unless there is a strong reason to switch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meeting agenda — about 30 minutes

| Time | Topic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Check-in and evidence volume: "What got evaluated?" | Overview / Deck confidence |
| 8 min | Track progress vs Goal — the same Track as last week is normal | Overview |
| 8 min | Themes from Prep and Review, plus the practice plan for next week's meetings | Journal |
| 5 min | One meeting deep-dive | Track analysis |
| 4 min | Commitments for next week; edit the Track only if needed | Overview configure / edit |

## Between meetings

A light mid-week Journal check.

## Red flags

- A Pending Skill or Goal for more than a few days.
- No recent evaluations — confidence dropping, and weekly emails may pause.
- Switching Skills every week, so the two-week minimum never applies.
- Prep themes ignored week after week.
