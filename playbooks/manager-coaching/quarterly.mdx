<Info>
  Level 03 of the [Manager Coaching Playbook](/playbooks/manager-coaching/overview).
</Info>

**About 45 to 60 minutes, once per Cycle.**

Best when your team is senior or self-directed, or when your span of control is large.

## Who owns the loop

The member and the Skill Deck autopilot. The manager provides Cycle-level oversight.

## Fit with Tracks

Expect several Tracks per Cycle, or fewer longer ones. Review completions, switches, Cycle rolls, and any Track still active into the next quarter.

## When to hold the meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preferred">
    The last two weeks of the Cycle, or the first week of the next Cycle once Wrap-Ups and report callouts have landed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional async">
    Skim a Track Wrap-Up in the Journal when it appears. No full meeting is needed unless something looks off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Out of band">
    Intervene on red flags without waiting for quarter end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prep checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the Cycle">
    Which Cycle are you reviewing — calendar quarter, or custom quarter dates?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team page">
    Cycle stats and the member Tracks timeline for that Cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Member Overview">
    Completed vs switched, rolled, and expired Tracks. Any still-active Track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Journal">
    The Wrap-Ups, plus a sample of Preps and Reviews across the Cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deck">
    Current scores and confidence, and the quarterly report callout if shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide next Cycle focus">
    Choose the focus Skills — whitelist, or start a Track.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meeting agenda — about 45 to 60 minutes

| Time | Topic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | Cycle story: what changed on the Deck this quarter? | Deck + Overview |
| 15 min | Tracks this Cycle — completed, still active, switched, rolled. What worked? | Overview + Journal Wrap-Ups |
| 10 min | Evidence habits — enough signal across the quarter? | Confidence / evaluation volume |
| 15 min | Next Cycle: one or two focus Skills, whitelist if needed, Target optional | Track Settings / Start Track |
| 5 min | Accountability: the evidence plan until the next quarterly review | Calendar / integrations |

## Between meetings

- Do not run weekly or monthly coaching by default.
- Watch the team page for stuck states, and temporarily move those members to Monthly or Weekly.
- Prefer a manager-managed whitelist plus auto Skill and Goal, so focus stays role-aligned without frequent meetings.

## Success signals

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tracks reach an outcome" icon="check">
    Multiple Tracks completed per Cycle, or fewer longer Tracks with clear progress and honest outcomes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confidence is not hollow" icon="check">
    Scores are backed by recent evidence across the quarter.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The member is self-directing" icon="check">
    They can state the next Cycle focus without you assigning every detail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sustained activity" icon="check">
    The team timeline shows steady activity, not only end-of-quarter spikes.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Red flags

- The Cycle ends with zero completed Tracks and little progress on any active Track.
- A long stretch with no open Track.
- Expired or stale Tracks.
- Evidence drops off for weeks — the autopilot emails pause, and a quarterly meeting has nothing durable to discuss.
- Constant switches that never reach the two-week completion window.
