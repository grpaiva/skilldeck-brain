## When to use this

Any time you write something a person outside the company will read: a landing page, an email, a deck, a social post, a support reply, or an answer from an AI agent.

## Prerequisites

Read [What is Skill Deck?](/company/what-is-skill-deck) and check [Canonical facts](/overview/canonical-facts) for any number you plan to state.

## The core message

> Skill Deck creates an individual behavioral profile for each employee based on what they do, not on what they say or on third-party perceptions.

Every piece of copy supports that sentence or is off-message. Test any draft against it before shipping.

## The demonstration that works

Whenever there is room, show the granularity instead of describing it.

<CodeGroup>

```text Everyone else
The employee needs to develop their communication skills.
```

```text Skill Deck
You scored 8/20 in Storytelling. That means you can use specific
details to make a story more concrete. Your next level, 9, adds
narrative pacing: alternating tension and relief, and using pauses
to let the important moments land.
```

</CodeGroup>

This lands harder than any adjective. Lead with it in demos, decks, and landing pages. The number is the basis for a Track Goal. When the same levels appear as resource cards, hide the number so the person uses the behavior when the opening comes, not because it is a higher score.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "This is surveillance." | No video, no audio, transcripts gone in 7 days, and only the member's own behavior is evaluated. Answer from [Privacy and data](/product/privacy-and-data), not from instinct. |
| "Our people will not want a bot in their meetings." | The bot is visible and the user controls their own calendar connection. Say what is actually true for the current build — check the privacy page. |
| "We already have a performance review tool." | Those tools collect self-reported and manager-reported input, which is the perception problem. We change the input, not the form. |
| "We already use a meeting recorder." | Recorders stop at the transcript. They have no model of the person and no development cycle. |
| "Can an AI really judge a person?" | It does not judge a person. It scores observed behavior against a defined level, cites the evidence, and attaches a confidence score that says how much evidence is behind it. If it saw nothing relevant, it records nothing. |
| "How long until we see something?" | A Track runs about two weeks minimum, and the trial is a month, because confidence needs evidence to accumulate. Do not promise value in a week. |
| "Our managers do not have time for another tool." | The weekly loop runs on autopilot without them. The Manager Coaching Playbook offers a quarterly cadence of one 45-minute meeting per person per Cycle as a legitimate way to use it. |

## Words

    Skill. Evidence. Observed behavior. Level. Track. Goal. Cycle. Deck. Confidence. Development cycle. Behavioral profile. Frictionless.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Avoid" icon="x">
    Competency. Attribute. Powerful. Seamless. Robust. Revolutionary. AI-powered. Game-changing. Surveillance. Monitoring. Grading. Ranking. Judging. AI interviews. Activities (as a live feature).
  </Card>
</Columns>

**Frictionless** is approved and it means something specific: the employee connects a calendar and keeps working, and everything else reaches them by email. Use it about that experience. It is not a general adjective for the product, and it is not a license to bring back "seamless".

"Monitoring" and "surveillance" are worth calling out separately. Never use them about our own product, even to deny them — repeating the frame reinforces it. Say what the product does instead.

Use the [glossary](/overview/glossary) terms exactly. Inventing a synonym for an existing concept costs us consistency across every surface.

## Claims that are off limits

<Warning>
  Do not state any of the following without a founder approving the specific wording.
</Warning>

- **Any number not on [Canonical facts](/overview/canonical-facts).** Especially revenue, ARR, customer count, seats sold, retention, or accuracy percentages. None of those exist in this brain. Do not estimate them.
- **Customer names or logos.** We have no approved reference list.
- **Comparative claims about named competitors.**
- **Scientific or psychometric validity claims.** Skill Deck is not a validated psychometric instrument, and we do not describe it as one.
- **Compliance certifications.** Do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or any certification unless it is documented here. It is not.
- **Guaranteed outcomes.** No promises about promotion, retention, performance improvement, or time saved.
- **Fundraising status.** See [Stage and fundraising](/company/stage-and-fundraising).
- **Activities or AI interviews.** Skill Deck evaluates real work evidence. Do not describe practice sessions the product runs.
- **A guided onboarding flow.** Onboarding is off for now.
- **Why we skip 250+ companies.** The reason is IT and compliance policy on meeting content and meeting bots, not HRIS, procurement, or a longer sales cycle. Do not invent a buyer-process story. On-customer processing is planned, not live.
- **Email, documents, files, or calls as evidence.** Never implemented. Do not list them next to meetings.
- **Connect Slack first.** Slack is implemented. Do not ask new customers or test teams to connect it at the start. Do not promise Teams chat or other messaging platforms.

## Definition of done

- Every number traces to [Canonical facts](/overview/canonical-facts).
- Every product claim traces to a `verified` page in the Product section.
- Nothing on the off-limits list appears.
- The copy still supports the core message.
- No word from the Avoid column survived the final read.

## Failure modes

**An agent fills a gap with a plausible number.** This is the most common and most damaging one. Any specific figure that is not on the facts page was invented. Delete it; do not soften it.

**Describing a planned feature in the present tense.** Check the page's `status` and any inline "planned" labels before writing about a capability.

**Answering a privacy question from memory.** Privacy answers are load-bearing for the deal and for our credibility. Always open [Privacy and data](/product/privacy-and-data).

**Improvising manager guidance.** If someone asks how a manager should use Skill Deck, the answer is in the [Manager Coaching Playbook](/playbooks/manager-coaching/overview). Do not invent a cadence, an agenda, or a best practice that contradicts it.
