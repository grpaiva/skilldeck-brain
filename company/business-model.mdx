Skill Deck is a per-seat SaaS product sold in seat bundles. A team buys a plan, gets a fixed number of licenses, and pays monthly.

<Info>
  These are the live prices, confirmed by a founder on 2026-09-01. Plan and pricing records in the application's seeder files are **outdated** — ignore them.
</Info>

## Plans

| Plan | Licenses | Price / month | Effective per seat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small Teams | 5 | \$74.99 | \$15.00 |
| Growth | 15 | \$199.00 | \$13.27 |
| Scale Up | 40 | \$499.00 | \$12.48 |
| Enterprise | 100 | \$999.00 | \$9.99 |

Headline range: **\$9.90 to \$15.00 per user per month**, depending on plan.

<Note>
  The published range starts at \$9.90 but the Enterprise plan computes to \$9.99 per seat. That is an inconsistency in the source material, not a second price point. Quote the range as written; do not derive a \$9.90 tier from it.
</Note>

Larger bundles get cheaper per seat, so pricing pulls buyers upward. The steepest step down is between Scale Up and Enterprise, which makes 40 seats the number to push past in a negotiation.

## Free tier and quotas

Alongside the [one-month trial](/company/icp-and-market), the product bounds what a free account can do. Useful to know before promising anything on a call.

| Free allowance | Limit | Resets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting bots | 4 | Monthly |
| Slack channels | 1 | Never. Quota exists; do not ask new customers to connect Slack at the start. |
| Advisor Picks episodes | 1 | Weekly |
| Development reports | 1 | Monthly |
| Activity sessions | Unused | Leftover from a [legacy feature](/product/cycles-and-tracks) |

Calendar integration is free; the bound is on how many meetings get a bot. Paid plans lift all of these.

## Skill Deck Pro

An individual plan exists: **Skill Deck Pro**, at **\$19.90 / month** or **\$199.00 / year**. One plan, two billing intervals.

It is **not the commercial focus**. We sell to teams. We do not expect individuals to buy Skill Deck as it stands. A future recruitment feature set may change that — planned, not live. Keep Skill Deck Pro out of the default pitch.

## The trial

The one-month free trial in the [go-to-market plan](/company/icp-and-market) is a sales motion, not a product feature. A team uses the product on its real work and measures results on its own people before paying anything.

It runs on the team's actual meetings, which is the only way the product can show anything worth paying for.

## The target

**10,000 paid seats within the first 18 months**, in the first wave.

That is the number the [fundraise](/company/stage-and-fundraising) is sized against, and the number that determines whether the land-grab hypothesis held.

## Reading the economics

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Expansion is structural" icon="trending-up">
    Skill Deck is bought per seat by teams that grow. Expansion comes from adding people and from spreading to adjacent teams, not from upselling features.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cost scales with meetings" icon="gauge">
    Every seat generates evidence that has to be transcribed and evaluated by models. Unlike most SaaS, our variable cost tracks how much a customer actually uses the product.
  </Card>
</Columns>

That second point is worth understanding before quoting discounts. A heavy user costs materially more to serve than a light one, which is why the product meters usage internally.

## What needs resolving

- **Annual team pricing** and any annual discount. Neither exists yet. Skill Deck Pro already has an annual interval; team plans do not.
- **A discount policy.** Discounts are founder decisions, case by case.
