Skill Deck is a per-seat SaaS product sold in seat bundles. A team buys a plan, gets a fixed number of licenses, and pays monthly.

<Warning>
  **Our fundraising pricing and the pricing in the product do not match.** The September 2026 one-pager quotes roughly double what the application seeds, and the application also has a per-extra-seat price and an individual consumer plan that the one-pager does not mention.

  Do not quote a price to a prospect from this page until a founder resolves which set is real.
</Warning>

## Team plans — as stated in the fundraising one-pager

| Plan | Licenses | Price / month | Effective per seat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small Teams | 5 | $74.99 | $15.00 |
| Growth | 15 | $199.00 | $13.27 |
| Scale Up | 40 | $499.00 | $12.48 |
| Enterprise | 100 | $999.00 | $9.99 |

Headline range: **$9.90 to $15.00 per user per month**.

The published range starts at $9.90 but the Enterprise plan computes to $9.99 per seat. That is an inconsistency in the source material, not a second price point.

## Team plans — as seeded in the application

| Plan | Base / month | Seats included | Each extra seat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small Team | $39.90 | 5 | $9.00 |
| Growth | $99.00 | 15 | $7.00 |
| Scale Up | $199.00 | 40 | $6.00 |
| Enterprise | $399.00 | 100 | $5.00 |

Effective per-seat rates here run $3.99 to $7.98 — roughly half the one-pager. The model is also structurally different: seats beyond the bundle are billed individually rather than forcing an upgrade.

## Free tier and quotas

The one-pager describes a one-month free trial. The product actually runs a **permanent free tier with quotas**, which is a different motion and worth knowing before you promise anything.

| Free allowance | Limit | Resets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting bots | 4 | Monthly |
| Slack channels | 1 | Never |
| Advisor Picks episodes | 1 | Weekly |
| Development reports | 1 | Monthly |
| Activity sessions | 2 | Weekly |

Calendar integration is free; the bound is on how many meetings get a bot. Paid plans lift all of these.

No self-serve trial period is configured at checkout. "Complimentary Pro" is granted through manual admin subscriptions rather than a productized trial.

## The trial

The one-month free trial in the [go-to-market plan](/company/icp-and-market) is a sales motion, not a product feature. A team uses the product on its real work and measures results on its own people before paying anything.

It runs on the team's actual meetings, which is the only way the product can show anything worth paying for.

## The target

**10,000 paid seats within the first 18 months**, in the first wave.

That is the number the [fundraise](/company/stage-and-fundraising) is sized against, and the number that determines whether the land-grab hypothesis held.

## What needs resolving

- **Which price list is real.** The two above differ by roughly 2x. Until a founder decides, quoting either to a prospect is a risk.
- **Whether the individual plan is part of the story.** It exists in the product but appears nowhere in the fundraising material, and it cuts against a seats-based narrative built on 10,000 paid seats.
- **Whether the go-to-market is "free month then paid" or freemium.** The plan says trial; the product says permanent free tier with quotas. These attract different users and convert differently.
- Annual team pricing and any annual discount.
- A discount policy. Discounts are founder decisions, case by case.

<Warning>
  Do not quote a price, offer a discount, propose a custom tier, or extend a trial without a founder approving it.
</Warning>
