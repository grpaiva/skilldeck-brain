## Where we are

Skill Deck is **live and running in version 2.0**, built entirely by the two founders. Two people, no engineering hires, product in production.

We operate with a lean startup mindset: build fast, test, validate, and keep the burn small enough that being wrong is survivable.

<Info>
  Stage language: we are raising a **first round to validate the model**. This is not a growth round and should never be described as one.
</Info>

## What we are raising

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Amount | **$750,000** |
| Runway | **18 months** |
| Purpose | Prove the growth hypothesis, not scale it |
| Target at the end | **10,000 paid seats** |

The round is deliberately sized to test one thing: whether the land-grab motion described in [ICP and market](/company/icp-and-market) converts a free trial month into paid seats at the rate we think it does. If it holds, we raise a larger round and scale globally. If it does not, we have learned that for $750,000 rather than for $10 million.

## Use of proceeds

The money goes to three places.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Tools" icon="wrench">
    The infrastructure and model spend that running evaluations at volume requires.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Marketing experiments" icon="flask-conical">
    Paid tests to find which channels feed the land-grab motion across the US, Mexico, and Brazil.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Key hires" icon="user-plus">
    A small number of people to support global growth of the product. Deliberately few.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The shape of this matters when talking to investors: it is a spend plan for finding out whether the motion works, not a hiring plan for a team we already know we need.

## What we want from investors

We are explicit that we are not only raising money. We are looking for:

- **Connections** and market access, especially in the US.
- **Smart capital** — investors who will give us intellectual support on our real challenges, dilemmas, and decisions.

Two founders who have built one company before know exactly which parts they have not seen. That is what the ask is aimed at.

## How to talk about the stage

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Say this">
    - "We are raising our first round to validate the model."
    - "Two founders, product live in 2.0, built entirely by us."
    - "$750K for 18 months, targeting 10,000 paid seats."
    - "We want investors who can give us market access and intellectual support."
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Not this">
    - Anything implying the round is closed, oversubscribed, or committed.
    - Revenue, ARR, customer counts, or logos. Those numbers are not in this brain and must not be invented.
    - Valuation, terms, or instrument. Founders only.
    - Naming investors, prospective investors, or intro sources.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
