## The problem

Three things are true in almost every company we talk to.

**Leaders have no time to develop their teams.** Development is the thing that gets dropped when the quarter gets tight. It is important, never urgent, and permanently postponed.

**Feedback is hard to give.** Even managers who want to give it struggle, because giving useful feedback means remembering specific behavior weeks after it happened and finding a way to say it that lands.

**Performance reviews run on perception, not evidence.** The annual review is assembled from what people remember and what colleagues say. It is shaped by recency, by who is visible, and by who gets along with whom.

The result is that most people receive one round of generic feedback per year and nothing they can act on.

## What Skill Deck is

A Skill tracking and development tool for the people who work at a company.

Skill Deck analyzes 58 Skills from real work evidence and builds a map for each person that shows their strengths and their gaps. The behavioral profile is based on what someone does, not on what they say about themselves and not on a colleague's impression.

The evidence is the work itself, primarily the meetings people already have. Nothing needs to be logged, filled in, or self-reported.

## Why the granularity matters

This is the sharpest part of the pitch, and the easiest to state concretely.

We measure 58 Skills, each on a 0 to 20 scale. That resolution is what turns a platitude into an instruction.

<CodeGroup>
```text Typical feedback
The employee needs to develop their communication skills.
```

```text Skill Deck
You scored 8/20 in Storytelling. That means you can use specific
details to make a story more concrete. Your next level, 9, adds
narrative pacing: alternating tension and relief, and using pauses
to let the important moments land.
```
</CodeGroup>

A person can act on the second one tomorrow morning. That is the whole product in one comparison — use it early in any conversation.

## How a person experiences it

Every user has an individual development Track: one Skill, analyzed in detail over at least two weeks, from real work evidence. They see the level they are at now and receive the behaviors that move them to the next one.

Along the way they receive a Kickoff email, a Weekly Prep email, a Weekly Review, a Wrap-Up at the end of the Track, and an analysis after every meeting.

The tool is low friction by design. A user connects their calendar and goes back to work. Everything about their profile arrives in their inbox.

## The role of leadership

The tool does the analysis. The leader does the leading. We are explicit that the human role of the manager is essential, and we build for it rather than around it.

A manager gets a clear view of the team and of each individual: their Tracks, their recent evaluations, and what is proposed for their growth.

A manager can also whitelist the Skills they consider most relevant for a person — matched to their job scope, their personal challenges, or the organization's values.

At the end of each Track, month, or Cycle, the manager has real data to review both how the tool is being used and how the person is developing.

The manager's side is not left to improvisation. The **Manager Coaching Playbook** gives three cadences to choose from — Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly — with an agenda, success signals, and interventions for each.

## Unique value proposition

> Our competitive advantage is delivering what no one else offers end to end: assessment and analysis of observed behavior in real meetings, combined with a continuous development cycle over time.

The two halves are what matter. Plenty of tools do meeting intelligence. Plenty of tools do Skill frameworks and development plans. The combination — observed behavior feeding a development cycle that runs continuously — is the position we are claiming.

For HR, the outcome is the thing to lead with: HR can finally hand leadership a tool that puts development on the agenda and makes structured, individual feedback possible.

## The claim underneath everything

Skill Deck creates an individual behavioral profile for each employee based on what they do, not on what they say or on third-party perceptions.

If a piece of copy does not ultimately support that sentence, it is off-message.
