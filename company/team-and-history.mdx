## Descola

The prior company is the strongest proof point in the deck, so state it precisely.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| What | An online school in Brazil |
| How it was funded | Completely bootstrapped — zero initial capital, zero initial network |
| Profitability | Consistently profitable |
| Customers | Over 100 enterprise clients |
| Outcome | Became one of the leading companies in its sector in Brazil |

What it demonstrates is not a headline exit. It is that these two founders built something from nothing, sold it to enterprises, kept it profitable, and navigated the long middle where most companies quietly die. We got things right, got things wrong, and found the paths that kept the business viable over time.

It also explains the T&D market knowledge. Twelve years selling into HR and L&D is where the Skill Deck insight came from.

## Founder-market fit, stated plainly

Behavioral development is moving from perception to evidence, and Skill Deck is built for that shift. We are not guessing at it. Twelve years operating in this market taught us what HR buys and what managers do with a tool once it is bought, and it taught us why development programs fail.
