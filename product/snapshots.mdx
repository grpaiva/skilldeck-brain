A **Snapshot** recalculates a person's Deck from their evaluation history for a chosen period, with the scoring algorithm in force at creation time. Scores, confidence, and demonstration counts are frozen to that window. Managers and members use Snapshots to see how a profile moved across a training cycle, a quarter, or any other span they care about.

Skill Deck also generates Snapshots automatically at the end of each [Cycle](/product/cycles-and-tracks). Optional [Targets](/overview/glossary) that look at historical quarters use the same day rules.

## Period boundaries

Both picked days are **inclusive**, in **São Paulo time** (`America/Sao_Paulo`), for every user. The start is the start of the start day; the end is the end of the end day. Those bounds are stored as UTC.

A meeting at 22:00 on the end day belongs to that Snapshot. A following Snapshot that starts the next day does not count it again. A Q4 Snapshot covers 1 Oct 00:00 through 31 Dec 23:59:59 São Paulo time, so the last day of the quarter is neither dropped nor shown a day early.

Views and quarter lookups use those local dates. Track Snapshots still end at the moment they are taken.

## Compare

**Compare** puts two sides side by side: Snapshot vs Snapshot, Snapshot vs **Current Deck**, or either vs a [Benchmark](/overview/glossary). One radar and one Skills table. There is no category table.

Lookups stay scoped to the viewer. Snapshot ids must belong to the signed-in user; Benchmarks must be ones they can see.

### Current and Max

A **Current / Max** toggle switches what the radar and table show.

| Mode | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Current** | Each side's current score, with Change |
| **Max** | Each side's `max_score`. No Change label. A callout notes that Max rises with demonstration volume and shows each side's total demonstrations |

Max is only comparable between periods with similar volume.

### Skills table

Each Skill row shows, for both sides:

- the score, with a 95% margin (`± m`) when that side has at least two demonstrations;
- a confidence dot on the same bands as the Deck — **50%+** green, **30–49%** amber, **under 30%** red (see [Confidence and scoring](/product/confidence-and-scoring));
- demonstration count and distinct meeting count.

Skill names are translated. Columns are sortable. Skills without a score on a side are skipped on the radar — they are not drawn at 0.

### Change

| Label | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Improvement** / **Decline** | The absolute difference exceeds the combined margin `√(m_a² + m_b²)` |
| **Within noise** | The difference stays inside that combined margin |
| **Not enough data** | Either side has fewer than two demonstrations |

When one side has no score, Change is empty.

### URL state

The comparison lives in the URL so a link restores the same view: `a` and `a_type`, `b` and `b_type`, `mode` (`current` or `max`), plus `sort` and `dir`.

## Who creates them

Members create personal Snapshots. Managers create Snapshots for people they manage. Cycle-end automation creates the quarterly ones. See [The manager role](/product/manager-role).
