A **Snapshot** recalculates a person's Deck from their evaluation history for a chosen period, with the scoring algorithm in force at creation time. Scores, confidence, and demonstration counts are frozen to that window. Managers and members use Snapshots to see how a profile moved across a training cycle, a quarter, or any other span they care about.

Skill Deck also generates Snapshots automatically at the end of each [Cycle](/product/cycles-and-tracks). Optional [Targets](/overview/glossary) that look at historical quarters use the same day rules.

## Period boundaries

Both picked days are **inclusive**, in the **time zone of the person the Snapshot is created for**. That zone is detected from the browser and editable in Settings → Profile; it is São Paulo (`America/Sao_Paulo`) when none is set. Each Snapshot stores its zone. The start is the start of the start day; the end is the end of the end day. Those bounds are stored as UTC.

A meeting at 22:00 on the end day belongs to that Snapshot. A following Snapshot that starts the next day does not count it again. A Q4 Snapshot covers 1 Oct 00:00 through 31 Dec 23:59:59 in its time zone, so the last day of the quarter is neither dropped nor shown a day early.

Views and quarter lookups use those local dates. Track Snapshots still end at the moment they are taken.

## Compare

**Compare** is an admin page at `/snapshots/compare`. Only someone who can open the Skill Deck admin panel can load it; anyone else gets a 403. The app has no link to it: the navbar, the Snapshots list, and a Snapshot's page carry no Compare button, so the URL is the only way in.

Each side picks a person and one of that person's decks: their **Current Deck** or one of their Snapshots. Side B can instead be a [Benchmark](/overview/glossary). The two sides can be one person in two periods, or two different people. Any person's Snapshots and Current Deck can be selected. A Snapshot counts only when it belongs to the person picked on that side, and Benchmarks must be ones the viewer can see.

Planned — not shipped as of 2026-11-17: limiting the people a viewer can pick to themselves and the people they manage, before Compare is linked in the app.

### Page layout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Side cards">
    Side A and side B each have a person picker and a deck picker. A line under each picker shows the title, period, demonstrations, and meetings. A **Swap** button swaps the people, the decks, and the score sort. It is disabled while side B is a Benchmark.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Radar and summary">
    The radar sits beside a summary: the count of Skills that improved and declined (or are ahead and behind), each of which filters the table on click; the average score per side over the Skills scored on both sides, with its difference; demonstrations and meetings per side, where a meeting that scored several Skills counts once; average confidence per side; and the three biggest gaps each way, which open that Skill's row. A Benchmark comparison shows the radar full width with no summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Context Analysis">
    A card that explains where each side's scores come from. See [Context Analysis](#context-analysis).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skills table">
    A search box, a category filter, and in Current mode a change filter (All, Changed, Positive, Negative) with counts. Filters narrow only the table. The radar and the summary always use every Skill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Current and Max

A **Current / Max** toggle switches what the radar and table show.

| Mode | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Current** | Each side's current score, with Change, the full summary, and Context Analysis |
| **Max** | Each side's `max_score`. No Change label and no Context Analysis. The summary keeps the averages and the totals. A callout notes that Max rises with demonstration volume and shows each side's total demonstrations |

Max is only comparable between periods with similar volume.

### Skills table

Each Skill row shows, for both sides:

- the score;
- a confidence dot on the same bands as the Deck — **50%+** green, **30–49%** amber, **under 30%** red (see [Confidence and scoring](/product/confidence-and-scoring));
- demonstration count and distinct meeting count.

Column headers use each person's first name when the sides are two people, and the Snapshot title when they are one person. Skill names are translated. Columns are sortable. A Skill can sit in several categories, so the category is a filter, not a grouping. Skills without a score on a side are skipped on the radar — they are not drawn at 0.

### Context Analysis

A demonstration scores its stage base plus one point for each [context bonus](/overview/glossary) present: **Pressure** and **Complexity** describe the situation, and **Effect** describes what the person's contribution caused later in the meeting. A harmful behavior scores 0.

In Current mode, the Context Analysis card pairs an A bar and a B bar for the share of demonstrations with Pressure, with Complexity, and with Effect. Below them it shows each side's stage mix, with harmful behavior counted as 0. Each Skill row expands to the same detail for that Skill. The breakdown explains a score difference; the score and the Change label come from the scores alone. Max mode and Benchmark comparisons leave the card and the row detail out.

### Change

Change follows the score.

| Label | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Improvement** / **Decline** | One person on both sides. The right side scores higher (Improvement, green) or lower (Decline) than the left |
| **Ahead** / **Behind** | Two people. The person on the left is the basis. Ahead (green) means they score higher than the other person; Behind means lower |

When one side has no score, or the two scores are equal, Change is empty.

### URL state

The comparison lives in the URL so a link restores the same view: `a_user` and `b_user` (the person on each side; a Snapshot id without a person selects that Snapshot's owner), `a` and `a_type`, `b` and `b_type`, `mode` (`current` or `max`), `sort` and `dir`, `search` (Skill name), `category`, and `show` (`all`, `changed`, `positive`, or `negative`; Current mode only).

## Who creates them

Members create personal Snapshots. Managers create Snapshots for people they manage. Cycle-end automation creates the quarterly ones. See [The manager role](/product/manager-role).
