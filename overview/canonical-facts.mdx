## Numbers we do not have

Stating this explicitly is the point of the page. **None of the following exist in this brain, and none may be invented, estimated, or implied.**

- Revenue, ARR, MRR
- Customer count, seats sold, logos
- Retention, churn, conversion rate, activation rate
- Evaluation accuracy or model accuracy
- Unit economics: CAC, LTV, gross margin, cost per user
- Valuation, round terms, instrument, investor names
- Headcount plans or hiring targets beyond "key hires"
- Any compliance certification

<Warning>
  Skill Deck holds no compliance certifications documented here. Do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR certification, or equivalent. Skill Deck is also not a validated psychometric instrument.
</Warning>
