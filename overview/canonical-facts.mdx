| Billing | USD | BRL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | \$19.90 / month | R\$99.90 / month |
| Annual | \$199.00 / year | R\$999.00 / year |

Skill Deck Pro exists and is active. It is **not the commercial focus**. The ICP is teams; we do not expect individuals to buy the product as it stands. A future recruitment feature set may make an individual plan interesting — that is planned, not live. Do not lead with Skill Deck Pro in sales, investor, or marketing copy.

Free-account allowances: 4 meeting bots per month, 1 Slack channel, 1 Advisor Picks episode per week, 1 development report per month, 1 Superpower generation, and 4 Highlight generations. A paid seat, individual or through an organization, makes all of them unlimited. The Slack-channel quota exists; still do not ask new customers to connect Slack at the start.
