<Info>
  English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, French and Italian are the UI languages in production, and their columns match the app. The German, French and Italian columns come from desk research (Microsoft terminology, Apple's shipped strings, the Microsoft style guides and the HR software sold in each market) and are waiting for native review. Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese are **Planned — not shipped as of 2026-11-12**; their columns come from the same desk research.
</Info>

What each term means lives in the [glossary](/overview/glossary). This page holds the words. The `skilldeck-glossary` skill carries the same tables for AI agents. It ships with the team's `skilldeck-languages` skills, which install in Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and Cursor. A term changes here, in that skill, and in the app's translation files together.

## How each language speaks

Every language is written natively, sentence by sentence in its own shape. Each one picks its address form once and keeps it on every surface: the app, email, Advisor text and the AI prompts.

| Language | Variety | The reader is addressed as | `7,631` is written |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| English | American English | you | 7,631 |
| Portuguese | Brazilian Portuguese | você | 7.631 |
| Spanish | Neutral Latin American Spanish | tú | 7.631 |
| German | Germany | du | 7.631 |
| French | France | vous | 7 631, with a narrow no-break space |
| Italian | Italy | tu | 7.631 |
| Japanese | Japan | です・ます, with the reader left unnamed | 7,631 |
| Korean | South Korea | 해요체, with the reader left unnamed | 7,631 |
| Traditional Chinese | Taiwan, with Taiwanese vocabulary | 你 | 7,631 |

## The word for Skill

Each language uses the word its workplaces use most for a behavior someone can get better at.

| Language | Skill |
| --- | --- |
| English | Skill |
| Portuguese (Brazil) | habilidade |
| Spanish | habilidad |
| German | Kompetenz |
| French | compétence |
| Italian | competenza |
| Japanese | スキル |
| Korean | 역량 |
| Traditional Chinese | 技能 |

Japanese and Taiwanese workplaces keep コンピテンシー and 職能 for HR frameworks, so the product uses スキル and 技能.

## How the terms are used

- **Skill Deck and Skill Deck Pro** are written in Latin letters in every language, exactly as in English.
- **Deck, Advisor and Advisor Pick** stay in Latin letters in Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Traditional Chinese. Japanese and Korean write them in their own script, as in the table.
- **Goal and Target** each have their own word in German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese. Portuguese and Spanish use meta for both.
- **Scores** are written `8/20`, on a scale of 0 to 20, in every language.
- **Confidence** is explained the first time it appears, as how much evidence stands behind the score. Low confidence reads as "not enough evidence yet".
- **The meeting bot transcribes.** Copy describes it with each language's word for transcribing.
- **Stage labels** are methodology words. They feed the AI prompts, and member-facing text describes the behavior instead.
- **Category names** match the table in the app's `category-model.php` files. The 58 Skill names are translated with each language's content.
